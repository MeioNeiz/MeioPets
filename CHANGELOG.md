# Meio Pets

## 1.1.0

- Pet mode warning: a line when your pet is on Aggressive in a dungeon (with a sound) or on
  Passive while you're alone
- Hunters: a reminder to feed after the fight when your pet drops below happy, with the
  damage it gains from being fed
- Hunters: when you level and your pet is 2 or more levels behind, a line with its level
  and how far it is to the next
- Warlocks: a line 15 seconds and a sound 3 seconds before a subjugated demon breaks free
- Warlocks: a warning when your Succubus has Lesser Invisibility on autocast (on Forever a
  mob it hits first from invisibility evades)
- New settings, off at first: Growl, Torment or Suffering on autocast in a group with a
  tank; a feed button and key binding; a low food warning
- Shorter setting names, so none are cut off

## 1.0.1

- With FlareUI loaded, the no pet reminder now stays quiet only where FlareUI's own pet
  reminder would show: with its Buff Reminders or pet reminder off, or scoped to dungeons
  or raids while you are elsewhere, MeioPets reminds you
- An enslaved demon breaking free is no longer reported as died: "<name> broke free",
  without the died sound

## 1.0.0

- First release: a red pulse around the pet frame when your pet's health runs low (with a
  sound whenever the game lets addons read pet health), a line and a sound when your pet
  dies, and a reminder to call, revive or summon it after the fight
