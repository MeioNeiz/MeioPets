# Meio Pets

## 1.0.1

- With FlareUI loaded, the no pet reminder now stays quiet only where FlareUI's own pet
  reminder would show: with its Buff Reminders or pet reminder off, or scoped to dungeons
  or raids while you are elsewhere, MeioPets reminds you
- An enslaved demon breaking free is no longer reported as died: "<name> broke free",
  without the died sound

## 1.0.0

- First release: a red pulse around the pet frame when your pet's health runs low (with a
  sound whenever the game lets addons read pet health), a line and a sound when your pet
  dies, and a reminder to call, revive or summon it after the fight
